About this guide

Every object, sheet, document and task in E1 has a status. Statuses come in status sets: five fixed categories (New, Active, Resolved, Closed, Removed) with the sub-statuses your company or project adds under them. Values have a status of their own, from Assumed to Final, and anything can be put on hold with a reason.

Typical workflow

  1. Make a status set with your sub-statuses, and choose it for objects and sheets.
  2. Set an object's status on its status chip.
  3. Mark the values you have checked Confirmed; a change makes them Preliminary again.
  4. Put on hold what waits for someone else, and find it in the query view.
  5. Make a workflow from a template, assign it to instruments, and move them on their chip or on the board.
  6. Give a step exit criteria and an approval; a lead can override a failing criterion with a reason.
  7. Follow the progress on the dashboards, and open the items behind any number.
  8. Give the project a plan and follow it on the S-curve and the trends.

Status sets and the project's statuses

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-statusesRoadmap 33.1Review checks S1

Every object, sheet, document and task has a status from a status set: the five categories New, Active, Resolved, Closed and Removed, with sub-statuses a set adds under them. Configuration → Statuses makes a set and chooses the one each level reads.

Where
Configuration → Workflow → Statuses
Who
Everyone who can view the project sees statuses. Making a project's status set and choosing the sets: Manage project workflows (Project administrator); a company set: Edit the company library. With Editor Basic the section is read only.

Make a status set

#ActionExpected result
1Open Configuration → Statuses and click New status set…. Type its name, for example Deliverable, and tick what it is for: Objects, Sheets.The five categories are listed, each with Add under ….
2Click Add under Active and type Waiting for vendor data.—
3Choose For projects: All projects (the company library), or, with a project's name clicked in the Projects pane, Only that project, and click Save and publish.The set is listed under Status sets with its statuses in order.
4Under The project's status set per level, choose Deliverable for Objects and Sheets.Objects and sheets read it from now on. An item keeps its status; one the set does not have shows its first status, New.
  • The five main statuses cannot be removed or given another category; their names may change. Statuses are kept in category order.
  • Tasks read the task set: Open, In review, Done, Removed. A task is set Open or Done on its row in Tasks.
  • Built-in sets that their topics set are listed too: the revision, markup and construction sets.

Who

#ActionExpected result

Who

Workflows: steps an item moves through

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-workflowsRoadmap 33.2Review checks S3

A workflow is the statuses of a status set in order, its steps, with a credit, a time limit and a lock each may have. Make one from a template (Engineering deliverable, ISO 19650 deliverable, Instrument data, Diagram, Engineering object) in Configuration → Workflows.

Where
Configuration → Workflow → Workflows
Who
Making a project's workflow: Manage project workflows (Project administrator); a company workflow: Edit the company library. With Editor Basic the section is read only.
#ActionExpected result
1Open Configuration → Workflows and choose Instrument data in From template.It is listed: New → In design → Ready for check → Vendor data → Closed (locked), and its status set is under Statuses.
2Ask Alex to change a step, for example to give In design a limit of 10 days and two reasons to move back.Alex saves a new draft; publishing it makes version 2.
  • Each step is a status of the set, once; Removed is never a step, it is always reachable. A step's credit never decreases down the list.
  • A locked step keeps its items' values, name, tag and owner as they are until the item moves back; holds, comments and tasks stay possible. Rules leave a locked item's values as they are.
  • A step may make a task about the item, set a value, or notify the people it names, when an item enters it.
  • Moves, holds and value statuses are events of their own in the integration API's change feed: object.status-changed, object.hold-changed, value.maturity-changed.

Move an item to another step

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-moveRoadmap 33.2Review checks S3, S10

On the status chip an item with a workflow moves: to the next step, back to an earlier one with a reason, to Removed and back with a reason. A held item does not move forward; in a locked step its values, name and tag wait until it is moved back. In a query view, Move to… moves the selected rows' items at once.

Where
The status chip on an object's window; the board; Alex for many at once
Who
Move items in their workflow (Engineer and above)
#ActionExpected result
1Click an instrument's chip and choose Move to In design.The chip reads Active ▸ In design and Instrument data · 0 days.
2Ask Alex to move the 40 instruments to Ready for check.They move as one change; Alex lists any that did not move and why (on hold, for example).
3On the chip choose Move back to In design… and type a reason.It moves back; the reason is kept and the return is counted.
4Move it on to Closed and type a new description.Refused: it is in a locked step of its workflow. Move it back to change it.
5Choose Remove…, then Restore to …, each with a reason.Removed, then back in the step it left.
6In a query view of the tanks, select several rows (Ctrl-click), right-click and choose Move to…. Pick In design and Move.The list offers the steps they may move to, each with how many may. Those that can move move together as one change (Ctrl+Z undoes it); the rows that did not move are listed with why: on hold, at an approval, following no workflow, criteria not met.

Exit criteria: what an item needs before it moves on

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-criteriaRoadmap 33.3Review checks S4

A step may say what an item needs before it moves on: required values filled in, values confirmed and not on hold, related items far enough, no open errors, a revision issued, a checklist ticked, a condition. The status chip lists them with ticks and crosses; an item whose criteria all pass shows Ready to move.

Where
The status chip: To move on; Alex for a workflow's steps
Who
Everyone who can view the project sees them; ticking a checklist: Move items in their workflow
#ActionExpected result
1Ask Alex to give the step In design of Instrument data the criterion Description confirmed (value status at least Confirmed, not on hold), and to publish it.The workflow's next version has it.
2Open an instrument in In design whose description is Assumed and click its chip.To move on lists ✗ Description confirmed: 1 fails and why (Description is assumed).
3Ask Alex to move the 40 instruments to Ready for check.The ones whose description is confirmed move; Alex lists the others with the values that hold them back.
4Mark the description Confirmed and open the chip again.✓ and Ready to move.
  • A criterion either blocks (✗) or only warns (!): a warning does not stop the move.
  • A checklist criterion shows its items with boxes to tick on the chip.
  • A sheet's criterion can need its markups far enough: for example every markup on it Closed before the sheet moves to In check. An object's reads the markups on the sheets it is placed on.
  • Calculated values are left out of a value status criterion; a held value fails one that says not on hold.

Approvals: sign or reject a step

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-approvalsRoadmap 33.3Review checks S4

A step with an approval moves on only by its signatures: each sign-off step (Checked, Approved) is signed by someone who did not move the item into it, or sign an earlier step it names. A rejection with a reason sends the item back. An item changed after its approval shows Changed since Approved.

Where
The status chip: Approval
Who
Sign workflow approvals (Engineer and above, Reviewer), with a fresh sign-in. Alex never signs.
#ActionExpected result
1Move an instrument into a step with an approval and click its chip.Approval offers Sign Checked… and Reject…; Move to the next step is not there: the signatures move it on.
2As the person who moved it, click Sign Checked….Refused: someone else signs it.
3As a checker, click Sign Checked… and confirm the sentence (I have checked FT-3 in step Ready for check.).Signed; Sign Approved… is open now, for someone else again.
4As a lead, sign Approved.The item moves to the next step.
5Change one of its values.The chip shows Changed since Approved until it next moves forward.

Reject… asks why and sends the item to the step the approval names; the signatures given so far stay on record. The people a sign-off step names are notified when an item waits for them, and the person who moved it in is notified of a rejection.

Progress, weights and the plan

AvailableSince 2026-11-12WF-progressRoadmap 33.6Review checks S7

Progress is the credit of the step each item has reached, weighted by a number of your choice (or 1). Each workflow step's credit can be changed for the project. The plan gives the percentage progress should have reached by each date; the S-curve draws progress against it.

Where
Configuration → Workflow → Progress
Who
Manage project workflows (Project administrator)
#ActionExpected result
1Open Configuration → Progress.At the top, How progress is counted in three steps; below, one card per workflow under Credit per step, and The plan. With no workflow yet, Open Workflows takes you to make one.
2On the Instrument data card, type 70 in the box under Checked. Beside Each item weighs, choose a number attribute, or keep 1 (all items equal).The step is highlighted and its bar grows; Reset to … brings back the workflow's credit. An empty box keeps the workflow's credit.
3Under The plan, click Add a date three times and set 1 Dec 2026 – 10 %, 15 Jan 2027 – 50 %, 28 Feb 2027 – 100 %. Optionally type a Baseline name (B1).The curve beside the table draws the plan, with today marked. A date twice or a percentage lower than an earlier one is named on its row.
4In the bar at the bottom, click Save.The bar says Saved. While something needs fixing, Save is greyed out and the bar says how many things; Discard changes goes back to what was saved.
5On a dashboard, look at a Progress tile.The weighted share of the credits reached: an item in a step with credit 50 counts half.

Statuses in branches, and the Branches dashboard

AvailableSince 2026-11-12WF-branchesRoadmap 33.8Review check S12

An item's status, step and hold change in a branch like its values, and come to main with the push. Where main and the branch both changed a status, E1 proposes the lower one.

Where
A branch's Conflicts; Dashboards → Branches
Who
Everyone who works in branches; Lead engineers set a workflow's steps
#ActionExpected result
1In a branch, set a pump to Resolved; in main set it to Closed. In the branch, Pull from main.A conflict of kind Status, with Proposed: Keep mine (the lower status: resolved). Its category follows the status.
2In a branch, sign Checked on an item, then change one of its values in the branch.The signature is void: Checked is open to sign again.
3In a branch, move an item to a step its workflow marks main only, or give the last signature that moves it there.Refused: the step is entered in main, not in a branch.
4Give a step the criterion Not changed in an open branch and change an item in a branch.In main the item cannot move on until the branch pushes; the criterion names the branch.
5Open Dashboards → Branches.Open branches, In review, their age and open conflicts; branches by status: Open, In review, Merged (pushed and closed), Closed.

Dashboards compute on main. Ask Alex for a dashboard "in branch …" to compute it in one branch. Tasks, markups and construction status stay in main.
