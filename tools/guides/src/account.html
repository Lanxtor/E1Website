About this guide

This guide covers the LANXTOR E1 website at lanxtor.com as a customer uses it: logging in, your account page and each of its sections, getting E1 and choosing a license, the Academy with its courses and certificates, Alex the website assistant, the Shape E1 board, and the website's languages.

The usual order

  1. Log in or create an account: Log in and sign up.
  2. Find your way around the account page: The account page, then its sections Overview, Licenses, Usage, Organizations, Projects, Permissions and Billing.
  3. Get E1: Get E1, desktop installers and Licenses and prices.
  4. Learn E1: Academy courses, The course player, Certificates, The public certificate page, User guides and The Academy page.
  5. Ask and suggest: Alex on the website and The Shape E1 board; Languages.

Before you start

  • A web browser. No E1 installation is needed for the website.
  • Until the launch, the full website runs on a password-protected preview site; www.lanxtor.com shows a coming-soon page.
  • Several parts still show preview data: example data, not your own. They are marked Preview data only here, and the page itself says so in a banner. To look at the account page with preview data, open it with ?demo=1 at the end of its address; the preview person is "Alex Morgan" of "Acme Process Engineering".

The website says "Alex, the AI co-engineer" for E1's AI as a whole. Inside E1, Alex is the one assistant: the built-in reviewer, which can also get help from a language model when you tick it; see AI: Alex. The website's own chat assistant is also called Alex, and is different again: see Alex on the website.

What the section shows today

  • The Payment card. For an owner or billing administrator: "Online payment is coming soon. Until then, contact us to change your plan or seats." For anyone else: "Only owners and billing administrators of this organization manage payment."
  • The Invoices table; without billing rights, "Invoices are visible to the organization's billing administrators."

The billing provider and portal (roadmap W4.1) are not chosen yet. When they are, Manage billing opens the provider's portal to change plan, seats and payment.

How LANXTOR invoices you today

Invoices do not need the Account page: LANXTOR emails them to your organization's billing address (usually accounts payable; it does not need to be an E1 user), every month, quarter or year as agreed, with the invoice as a PDF.

#ActionExpected result
1Open the email Invoice LX-‹year›-‹number› from LANXTOR (‹amount›, due ‹date›).The invoice PDF is attached: LANXTOR's details, your billing details and reference, the lines, tax and total, and how to pay. The email gives the bank details and a link Pay by card.
2Pay by bank transfer, with the invoice number as the reference.The invoice shows LANXTOR's account in the invoice's currency, so for most countries it is a local transfer: an IBAN for euros, an account and routing number for US dollars, an account number and sort code for pounds. LANXTOR marks the invoice paid when the money arrives.
3Or open the Pay by card link.A page with the invoice, Download the invoice (PDF) and Pay ‹amount› by card, which opens the secure payment page of LANXTOR's payment provider (Stripe). After paying, the page says Paid.

To have invoices charged to a card automatically, ask LANXTOR: you get an email Set up card payment for E1 with a link to save a card on Stripe's page (valid 14 days). Each invoice is then charged on its date and emailed to you as paid (Receipt: invoice … paid). If the card is declined, you get the invoice with the pay link instead. LANXTOR never sees your card number.

To change the billing address, your reference (PO number) or how you pay, reply to any invoice email.

What you will be able to do

  • Click Manage billing and change plan, seats and payment method online.
  • Download your invoices on this page.

Alex on the website: ask and follow the suggestions

AvailableSince 2026-11-06Updated 2026-11-09WEB-assistantReview checks W1, W9

Ask the website's assistant anything about E1, by typing or by clicking one of its suggested questions, and it answers from the website's own information. After every answer it suggests 3 to 5 further questions in the direction you are going. No sign-in is needed. To have a request for information (RFI) answered, send it to sales@lanxtor.com.

Where
The floating Ask Alex button on every page; the ask bar on the home, solutions, Get E1 and Academy pages
Who
Anyone; it uses no organization credits

Steps

#ActionExpected result
1Click Ask Alex.A panel "Alex — Ask anything about E1. Answers are written by AI." with "Hi, I'm Alex. Ask me anything about E1…" and five suggested questions: "What is E1?", "What does E1 cost?", "Can E1 run on our own servers?", "What can Alex do in E1?" and "How does the 30-day trial work?".
2Click "What does E1 cost?"."Alex is writing" shows, then an answer with the prices, followed by new suggestions about prices, such as "What is the difference between Viewer, Reviewer and Editor?" and "How many AI credits are included?".
3Open another page of the website and click Ask Alex again.The conversation is still there (it is kept for this browser tab).
4Click a suggestion about Alex, then type a question of your own, such as "Do you have training courses?".Each answer is followed by 3 to 5 suggestions: first the follow-ups of what you asked (for Alex: "Can Alex make changes to my design?", "Is my data safe when I use Alex?" …), for a typed question the questions of its topic (here the Academy). Questions already asked are not offered again; from the second question on, the last suggestion is a next step such as "How do I get started?".
5Click New conversation.The greeting and the suggestions are back.

On a phone the panel fills the screen and its typing area stays right above the keyboard; tapping the ask bar on a page opens the panel straight away, ready to type. After you send a question the keyboard closes, so the answer and the suggestions have the screen.

The suggestions are in the page language. Alex on the website has no microphone and does not read answers aloud; type, or click a suggestion.

This assistant is not the Alex inside E1. It answers only from the website's text, may be wrong ("Answers are written by AI"), and keeps nothing of your conversation. Limit: 20 questions per 10 minutes per visitor ("That was a lot of questions in a short time…"). If it is not connected, it says "I'm not connected yet. …" and points to sales@lanxtor.com.
