About this guide

E1 Reviewer is E1's iPad app for reviewing projects away from the desk: on site, in a walk-down, in a design review. It opens the same E1 as the desktop app, with the same projects and roles, and does not change drawings. You search, ask Alex, look at sheets and objects, add photos and voice notes, redline sheets and work through the tasks and checklists assigned to you.

The usual order

  1. Sign in and choose the organization: Open it and sign in, Your profile and organization.
  2. Start from Home: The dashboard, Customize a dashboard, Projects as tiles.
  3. Review a project: Its sheets, An object or a connection, Photos and voice notes, Redline a sheet.
  4. Work through what is yours: For me. Find anything: Search, Alex.

Before you start

  • An iPad with iPadOS 15 or later and Safari, and an E1 account (or use Try the demo).
  • What you may do is what your license and project role allow in E1: a Viewer sees, a role that may comment writes in the chat and redlines.

E1 Reviewer is under development: it opens in Safari from your E1, the App Store app is not released yet, and these pages describe the version being tested with pilot customers.

Open E1 Reviewer on the iPad and sign in

Under developmentUpdated 2026-11-09IPAD-sign-in

E1 Reviewer opens in Safari on the iPad at your E1's own address, followed by /reviewer/. You sign in with your company account as in E1, and can add it to the Home Screen to open it like an app.

Where
Safari → your E1's address + /reviewer/
Who
Everyone with an E1 account
Before you start
Your E1 runs a version that has E1 Reviewer (ask your administrator).

Steps

#ActionExpected result
1In Safari on the iPad, open your E1's address followed by /reviewer/, for example app.lanxtor.com/reviewer/.The E1 Reviewer page shows Sign in and Try the demo.
2Tap Sign in and sign in with your company account, as in E1.E1 Reviewer opens on Home with Hello and your first name, your dashboard and your projects.
3Tap Share (the square with the arrow), then Add to Home Screen, then Add.The E1 Reviewer icon is on the Home Screen. Opened from there, it fills the screen like an app.
4To look around without an account, tap Try the demo instead.Home opens in the sample organization Demo Company; nothing you do there is sent anywhere.

The camera and the microphone ask for permission the first time you take a photo or record a voice note.

The E1 Reviewer app for the App Store is not released yet; in Safari it opens only your own E1 (the address you opened it from).

The dashboard

Under developmentUpdated 2026-11-09IPAD-dashboard

Home and each project's Overview show a dashboard of cards: your tasks, task status, checklists, projects, and in a project its 2D spaces, documents, pinned 2D spaces, redlines and recent changes.

Where
Home → Dashboard; a project → Overview
Who
Everyone
Before you start
You are signed in.

Steps

#ActionExpected result
1Look under Dashboard on Home.The line under the title says whose dashboard it is: Your own dashboard, Your organization’s dashboard or The standard dashboard.
2Tap a task in For me.For me opens on that task.
3Open a project and tap Overview.The project's dashboard shows 2D spaces (every 2D space, each with a pin), Documents, Pinned 2D spaces, For me, Redlines and Recent changes.
4In 2D spaces, tap the pin beside a 2D space.The pin fills in, and the 2D space shows in Pinned 2D spaces and at the top of the list on the left. Tap it again to unpin.
5In Documents, tap a document.Its latest issued revision opens as a PDF in a new tab. A document with nothing issued yet says so.

The most personal saved dashboard is shown: on Home yours, else the organization's; in a project yours for the project, else the project's, else your Home one, else the organization's.

Customize a dashboard for you, the project or the organization

Under developmentUpdated 2026-11-09IPAD-dashboard-configurator

Customize adds, removes, orders and sizes the cards and saves the dashboard for you, for everyone in the project, or for everyone in the organization.

Where
Dashboard → Customize
Who
Everyone for Just me; a project administrator for Everyone in the project; an organization administrator for Everyone in the organization
Before you start
Home or a project's Overview is open.

Steps

#ActionExpected result
1Tap Customize.The Customize dashboard panel opens with Save for, Cards and Add a card.
2Under Save for, choose Just me, Everyone in the project or Everyone in the organization.Only the levels you may save show. The cards are those of that level's dashboard.
3Change the size of a card (Small, Medium, Wide), move it with ↑ and ↓, or remove it with the bin.The list changes at once.
4Tap a card under Add a card.It is added at the end of Cards.
5Tap Save.The panel closes, a message says where it was saved, and the dashboard shows the cards.
6To go back to the shared dashboard, open Customize and tap Remove my dashboard.The project's or the organization's dashboard shows again.

Projects as tiles you arrange

Where
Home → Projects
Who
Everyone
Before you start
You are signed in.

Steps

#ActionExpected result
1Tap a tile.The project opens on its Overview.
2Back on Home, touch and hold a tile.The tiles start to wiggle, and Arrange reads Done.
3Drag the tile to another place.The other tiles make room as you move.

A project's sheets

Under developmentUpdated 2026-11-09IPAD-sheets

A project lists its sheets on the left, and the list can be hidden; the sheet shows as E1 draws it. One finger moves it, two fingers zoom; pin the sheets you use most.

Where
A project → Sheets
Who
Everyone with a role in the project
Before you start
A project is open.

Steps

#ActionExpected result
1Tap a sheet under Sheets.The sheet opens in the middle, fitted to the screen, with View and Redline above it.
2Pinch with two fingers.The sheet zooms about your fingers.
3Drag with one finger.The sheet moves.
4Tap Fit the sheet (the corners at the right of the bar).The whole sheet shows again.
5Tap the pin beside a sheet.The pin is highlighted and the sheet moves to the top of the list and into Pinned sheets on the project's dashboard.
6Tap Hide the sheet list (the panel button beside the project name).The list closes and the sheet fills the width. Show the sheet list, with the sheet's name at the top left, opens it again; the choice is kept.

An object or a connection: properties, attributes, history and chat

Under developmentUpdated 2026-11-09IPAD-object

Tap a symbol, a pipe, a signal or a cable on a sheet to see its object: properties, every attribute, its history and its chat.

Where
A sheet → tap a symbol or a line; the panel on the right
Who
Everyone with a role in the project; writing in the chat needs a role that may comment
Before you start
A sheet is open.

Steps

#ActionExpected result
1Tap a symbol.It turns orange and the panel on the right shows its tag, name and class, with Properties, Attributes, History, Chat and Photos & voice.
2Tap Attributes.Every attribute of the object's class, grouped, with its value and unit; empty ones show —. Filter attributes finds one by name or value.
3Tap History.The changes to the object, newest first, with who, when, and each value before and after.
4Tap Chat, write in Write a comment and tap Send.Your comment shows under the others and in E1's comments on the object.
5Tap a pipe, a signal or a cable.The panel shows its line object, for example its line number and fluid.
6Drag the handle between the sheet and the panel to the left or the right.The panel grows or shrinks and the sheet takes the rest; the width is kept for next time. A double tap on the handle sets it back.
7Tap Close object (the ✕).The panel closes.

Photos and voice notes

Under developmentUpdated 2026-11-09IPAD-photos-voice

Take a photo with the iPad's camera or record a voice note and add it to an object, a comment or a task; they are kept with the object's files in E1.

Where
Object panel → Photos & voice; Photo and Voice under every comment field
Who
Everyone with a role in the project that may edit the object (adding files)
Before you start
An object is open in the panel, or a task in For me.

Steps

#ActionExpected result
1Tap Photos & voice, then Take photo.The camera opens; take the photo and tap Use Photo. A message says Photo added and the photo shows.
2Tap Record voice.The first time, iPadOS asks to use the microphone. The button turns red and reads Stop with the time.
3Tap Stop.A message says Voice note added and the note shows with a player.
4In a comment, tap Photo or Voice before Send.The files wait above the field; Send sends them with the comment.

In E1, a comment with photos or voice notes lists them on lines of their own, and the files are in the object's files.

Redline a sheet

Under developmentUpdated 2026-11-09IPAD-redlining

Redline draws on a sheet with a pen, a cloud, an arrow or text in four colors; each mark says what it means and carries a note, and its status can be set.

Where
A sheet → Redline
Who
Everyone with a role in the project that may comment; setting another person's status needs a role that may resolve comments
Before you start
A sheet is open.

Steps

#ActionExpected result
1Tap Redline.The tools show: Pen, Cloud, Arrow, Text, the colors, and Remove my last redline.
2Choose Cloud and drag over the part of the sheet.A cloud follows your finger; when you lift it, Note for this redline opens.
3Choose what it means (Remark, Question, Add or change, Delete, Verified on site), write a note and tap Save redline.A message says Redline saved; the cloud stays on the sheet.
4Choose Text and tap the sheet.Text on the sheet opens; type the text and tap Save redline. Save redline stays off until there is text.
5Tap View, then tap the line of a redline.Its card shows who drew it, when, its note, and Status: Open, Accepted, Rejected, Answered, Incorporated, Closed.
6Tap Show redlines (the layers button).The redlines are hidden or shown.

Two fingers still move and zoom the sheet while Redline is on.

E1's sheet view on the desktop does not show these redlines yet; redlining in E1 itself is planned.

For me: your tasks and checklists

Under developmentUpdated 2026-11-09IPAD-for-me

For me lists the tasks and checklists assigned to you in every project; tick the items, add items, comment with photos and voice notes, and mark a task done.

Where
Top bar → For me
Who
Everyone; ticking needs the task to be yours or a role that may edit it
Before you start
You are signed in.

Steps

#ActionExpected result
1Tap For me.Your open tasks, the next due first, with the project, the due date and how much of each checklist is ticked. The number on For me counts your open tasks.
2Tap Overdue, Done or All.The list shows only those tasks.
3Tap a task.It opens on the right: its description, Checklist with the items, and Comments.
4Tap an item.It is ticked with your name and the time; the count above the list goes up.
5Type in Add a checklist item and tap Add.The item is added at the end of the checklist.
6Write a comment, add a Photo or a Voice note, and tap Send.The comment shows with its photos and voice notes.
7Tap Mark done.The task moves to Done and the number on For me goes down. Open again reopens it.

E1's task view on the desktop does not show checklists yet.

Alex on the iPad

Under developmentUpdated 2026-11-09IPAD-alex

Alex on the iPad is Ask: you ask about the project, an object or a practice, typed or spoken, and Alex answers without changing anything.

Where
Top bar → Alex
Who
Everyone with a role in the project
Before you start
Your installation has Alex's AI help (a language model) set up; speaking also needs its voice input.

Steps

#ActionExpected result
1Tap Alex in the top bar (Alex's blue gear).The Alex panel opens on the right: Ask: answers only, never changes anything.
2Type a question in Ask Alex and tap Ask.Your question and Alex's answer show in the conversation. Alex never changes the project here.
3Tap Speak your question (the microphone), say your question, then tap the red button with the time.The first time, the iPad asks to use the microphone. Your words appear in Ask Alex, to correct before Ask.
4Close the panel with ✕ and open it again.The conversation is still there.

The iPad has Alex's Ask mode only. Alex's review of a drawing, Agent and Plan, which change the project, and the E1 Guide are in E1 on the desktop.

Without a language model in your installation, Alex says that no AI model is set up; without voice input the microphone is greyed out.
