Deep view

Deep view: everything linked to an object, at a glance

Deep view shows an object and every object associated with it, as a data model of cards and links, like an object diagram rather than a drawing. You open it layer by layer with +1, or all at once with Expand all, until nothing more is linked.

From a valve you follow the pipes on its ports to the pump beyond, the transmitter that measures the pump, its terminals, the cable core landed on them, the cable and the junction box at its other end. Before you isolate, replace or test something, you see what it touches.

Click a card to see its values and links, with Open, Show in tree and Deep view of this. Highlight route draws the way from one card to another in bold while everything else fades; Find in view outlines the cards whose tag, name, class or value holds your text; a chip for each kind of link shows or hides those links.

  • Opened from the object window's Deep view tab, or from a symbol on a 2D space: right-click → Open ▸ Deep view.
  • The bar under the view counts the objects, links and levels shown, and the cards that have more to show.
Annotation

Redlining on the live diagrams

In E1, you mark up a 2D space with clouds, arrows, text, pins and stamps on a Markups layer that never changes the drawing, and each markup is discussed in its own card.

Every markup has a status and a response: accept, reject, answer, incorporate or close. Each change is written in its discussion and tells its author, so nothing raised on site is lost on the way back to the office.

The Markups list filters a 2D space's markups and runs a review round with its reviewers; when it is closed, the comment resolution sheet is exported. A marked-up print carries each markup as a real PDF comment, and a reviewer's annotated PDF reads back into E1 as markups in the round.

iPad and phone

On site with the iPad and the phone

E1 Reviewer for iPad and E1 Mobile for iPhone and Android launch with E1 on 19 May 2027, on the same model.

On the iPad, every drawing goes to site. Redline it with a pen, a cloud, an arrow, text or a stamp; each mark says what it means and carries a note, goes into a review round and is answered by the engineers in E1. Tap a symbol, a pipe, a signal or a cable to see its object: its properties, every attribute, its history and its chat.

On the phone, messages, tasks and Alex are in your pocket, typed or spoken. Everything lands in the same E1 model at once.

Tasks

Tasks, technical queries and engineering queries

In E1, a question about the plant is a task on the objects it is about, not an e-mail.

New task… offers E1's seven task types, including Technical query and Engineering query, and your organization's own, each a task or a query. A task has its due date and the person responsible, and it stays linked to the objects and drawings it concerns, so the answer is found where the question was asked.

Chat

A chat on every object

In E1, every object has a Chat tab: the conversation about a pump, a valve or a cable stays with that pump, valve or cable.

@mention a colleague to bring them in, #link another object, reply, like, edit, delete and resolve. Whoever opens the object later reads why it is the way it is.

Branches

Modifications and commissioning packages in branches

In E1, a plant modification is made in a branch while the shared design stays untouched, and finished work is collected into a commissioning package.

Collect finished work into a commissioning package by drawing around it or by right-click, give it its dates, lead and description, then mark it ready and push it to main on its own. Every change is recorded and compared before it lands.

Questions

Les questions que posent les ingénieurs.

What is Deep view in E1?

Deep view shows an object and every object associated with it as cards and links. You open it layer by layer or all at once, highlight the route between two objects and find any object in the view.

Can I raise a technical query in E1?

Yes. New task… offers Technical query and Engineering query among E1's seven task types, and your organization can add its own.

Can I redline drawings on site?

Yes. In E1 you mark up any 2D space on a Markups layer, and E1 Reviewer for iPad, launching on 19 May 2027, redlines on site with the marks answered by the engineers in E1.
