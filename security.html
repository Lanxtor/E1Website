Security.

Found a vulnerability in E1? Tell us. We welcome reports from security researchers and customers, and we work with you to fix the issue before it is made public.

How to report a vulnerability

Email support@lanxtor.com with the subject "Security vulnerability". Please include:

  • which product or address is affected, and its version if you know it;
  • what the vulnerability is and what an attacker could do with it;
  • the steps to reproduce it, and a proof of concept if you have one;
  • how we can reach you, and whether you want to be named when we publish the fix.

Please write in English, and do not put the details in a public issue, forum or social media post.

What is covered

  • E1 in the browser and the E1 Cloud (app.lanxtor.com)
  • The E1 desktop app for macOS and Windows
  • E1 Reviewer for iPad
  • This website (www.lanxtor.com)

Enterprise installations on a customer's own servers are covered for E1 itself; the customer's own systems and networks are not.

What we do

  • We confirm that we received your report within 2 working days.
  • We assess it, tell you whether we can reproduce it and how severe we find it, and keep you informed until it is fixed.
  • We fix it as quickly as its severity requires. The desktop app updates itself, so a fix reaches every installed app without a new download.
  • We agree with you when the vulnerability is made public, normally once the fix is available and at the latest 90 days after your report.
  • Where a vulnerability is actively exploited, we report it to the authorities as the law requires, and we inform affected customers.

Testing in good faith

If you act in good faith and follow these rules, we will not take legal action against you for your research:

  • Only test with your own account and your own data, or a trial organization. Do not access, change or delete other people's data; if you reach it by accident, stop and tell us.
  • Do not disrupt the service: no denial-of-service tests, no spam, no large automated scans.
  • No social engineering of our staff or customers, and no physical attacks.
  • Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before you tell anyone else.
