Tested every night

Every change to E1 is timed against fixed budgets, and a slower build fails. Every week a stress test runs 100 engineers at once and a one-hour soak test.

Live for everyone

A change by one engineer reaches everyone who has the project open in about a second, pushed by the server, with no reload.

Scales with the load

The E1 server adds capacity when it is busy and is always running, so there is no cold start. Health checks every few seconds replace a server that stops answering.

Security

Secure by design, layer by layer.

Some protections come from Microsoft Azure, others we built into E1 itself. Together they protect your data from sign-in to storage.

Sign-in

  • Sign-in by Microsoft Entra External ID, or with your company's Microsoft account. E1 keeps no passwords.
  • Owners and administrators confirm with a passkey before they can use their rights.
  • Sensitive actions, such as giving admin rights or exporting everything, ask you to sign in again if your last sign-in is older than 15 minutes.
  • Sessions end after 12 hours.

Encryption

  • HTTPS only, with HSTS: your browser never talks to E1 unencrypted.
  • The E1 server reaches the database over TLS with full certificate checks, and storage only over TLS 1.2 or newer.
  • The database, files, backups and Key Vault are encrypted at rest with AES-256 by Microsoft Azure, with keys managed by Microsoft.

Every organization on its own

  • Every record in the database belongs to one organization, and PostgreSQL row-level security lets E1 read only the rows of the organization you work in.
  • Automated tests check the isolation of every table in every build.

Private network

  • The database has no public address: only the E1 server reaches it, inside a private Azure network.
  • Files cannot be opened from the internet, and storage keys are switched off: access is by identity only.

Secrets and keys

  • Every secret is in Azure Key Vault, protected against deletion. The E1 server reads them with its own Azure identity.
  • The AI service needs no API key: E1 signs in to it as itself, so no key can leak.
  • Logs never contain passwords, tokens or keys.

Protected app

  • A strict Content Security Policy, and protection against cross-site requests and framing.
  • Limits on how often sign-in and other actions can be tried.
  • The E1 server checks the permission of every change, whoever or whatever asks for it.

Safe releases

  • The macOS app is signed and notarized by Apple, the Windows app signed with Azure Artifact Signing. Updates never go back to an older version.
  • Every build is scanned for leaked secrets and known vulnerabilities, with a software bill of materials.
  • A release reaches the E1 Cloud only after a person approves it.

Audit log

  • Sign-ins, failed sign-ins, members, invitations, licenses, roles, exports and passkeys are recorded. Administrators see them under Organization → Audit.
  • Every call to the AI is logged with its model and size, without the text of your messages.

AI under your control

  • Your organization sets Alex to on, answer only, or off.
  • Alex works with your permissions, never more. Restricted values are removed before anything is sent to a model.
  • Alex asks before it deletes more than 10 objects at once.

Backups and recovery

Nothing is lost.

Your data is backed up all the time, kept in more than one place, and you can take all of it out whenever you want.

Database, to any point in time7 days
Deleted or overwritten files14 days
Keys and secrets in Key Vault90 days

Database

  • Continuous backups by Azure Database for PostgreSQL: the database can be restored to any minute of the last 7 days.
  • The backups are also copied to Azure's paired region in the EU, so they survive the loss of a whole region.

Files and documents

  • Every file is stored three times, in three separate data centres of the region (zone-redundant storage).
  • Earlier versions of every file are kept, and deleted files can be brought back for 14 days.
  • Issued revisions are never removed.

Recovery targets

  • Most work that can be lost1 hour (RPO)
  • Time to restore the service8 hours (RTO)

Restoring from a backup is tested automatically in every build of E1.

Ask for a restore

Deleted something by mistake? Most changes can be undone in E1 itself, from the history of the object or the sheet. For anything more, the owner or an administrator of your organization writes to support@lanxtor.com with the time to go back to. We can restore the database to any point in the last 7 days, and deleted files from the last 14 days.

Take all your data with you

Owners and administrators export the whole organization at any time under Organization → Export everything: one archive with every table as open JSON lines and every file, up to 4 GB. The export is never refused for lack of storage, and it is recorded in the audit log.

After a trial ends, the organization becomes read-only: everything stays visible and can still be exported.

Questions

Questions security teams ask.

Can LANXTOR employees open my drawings?

No. LANXTOR support sees your account and billing data so that we can help with licenses and seats. The database does not give support access to your projects, objects, drawings or documents, and every change support makes is shown in your audit log.

Does my data leave the EU?

Your projects, files and backups stay in Azure data centres in the EU. Alex's default model, GPT-4.1, runs in Sweden Central in the EU. Only if an Editor chooses Claude Opus 5.5 in the chat may those requests be processed outside the EU. Your organization can switch Alex off.

What happens if an Azure data centre fails?

Files are stored in three separate data centres of the region, so the loss of one does not affect them. The database backups are also copied to Azure's paired region in the EU. Our target is to restore the service within 8 hours, with at most 1 hour of work lost.

How do I get my data out if we leave?

An owner or administrator exports the whole organization at any time under Organization → Export everything, in open formats: JSON lines for the data and the original files. Ask us at support@lanxtor.com to delete the organization afterwards.

Can we choose another region or our own cloud?

A US region is coming to the E1 Cloud. If your data must be in a specific place or under your own rules, run E1 as an enterprise installation on Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, Alibaba Cloud (coming, for mainland China) or your own servers, with your own PostgreSQL database.

Can we use single sign-on?

People with a company Microsoft account can sign in with it today. Single sign-on with SAML and user provisioning with SCIM are planned for enterprise customers; contact sales.
