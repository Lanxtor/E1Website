Desktop

The desktop app for Windows and macOS

E1 is a desktop app for Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and for Macs with Apple silicon on macOS 12 or newer. Both are signed and keep themselves up to date.

Download it from Get E1: your own system is offered first. The Windows app is signed by Lanxtor LLC; the Mac app is signed and notarized by Apple, so macOS shows no warning about an unidentified developer. At the first start you choose the server: Use E1 Cloud, or type the address of your organization's own server. You sign in with the same account as in the browser, and your projects are there.

In the desktop app, floating windows are real windows: take a tab out to work side by side or on a second screen, and it opens again where it was at the next start. The app looks for a new version when it starts and every four hours, downloads it in the background and asks you to restart. You never download an installer again, and an update never goes back to an older version.

Browser

E1 in the browser, with nothing to install

In E1, the browser is a full place to work, not a reduced viewer: open app.lanxtor.com or your organization's E1 address, sign in, and the same projects open with the same tools.

The browser suits any computer, including Linux, Intel Macs and Windows on Arm, which have no desktop installer. E1 Cloud is tested with current Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge. On the account page of the website, Open E1 in the browser takes you straight in.

E1 saves each change on the server as you make it. Your tabs, your selection and the rows you opened are kept for you on the server and come back after a reload; your appearance and panel widths are kept per browser. In the browser, floating windows open as pop-ups.

Review

Review from a laptop or a tablet

In E1, reviewers and viewers work in the real model, not in an exported PDF: a Reviewer can redline, comment and approve, and a Viewer sees everything read-only.

A Reviewer marks up a 2D space with clouds, arrows, text, pins and stamps on a Markups layer, and can never change the drawing. Each markup has a card with its discussion and status, and a review round ends with a comment resolution sheet. Viewers see every project, drawing, list and document they are given, in E1 itself. Clients and contractors invited to a project as guests view it for free.

On a tablet, E1 runs in the browser. Some screens are made for a finger: the field view of the wire moves shows each lift and land in large rows, and one tap ticks a move off with your name and the time. E1 Reviewer for iPad and E1 Mobile for iPhone are coming to the App Store, as Get E1 shows.

One project

The same project, the same account, wherever you are

In E1, the project lives on the server, not on a device: the desktop app and the browser are two ways into the same data.

Start a change at your desk in the desktop app and check it later from a browser in the plant or a meeting room. A change by one engineer reaches everyone who has the project open within seconds, pushed by the server, with no reload. Avatars on a 2D space's toolbar show who else has it open, and when two people change the same thing, the first change is kept and the second is refused with a clear message.

An E1 license is for one named person, and every change and approval is recorded under that name. One account may be open in up to three places at once, browsers and the desktop app together. In a fourth place, E1 asks which one to sign out.

  • Desktop app: Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit); macOS 12 or newer on Apple silicon.
  • Browser: current Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge, on any computer or tablet.
  • Servers: E1 Cloud, or your organization's own E1 installation.
  • Updates: the server and the desktop app each tell you when a new version is ready.
Questions

Questions engineers ask.

Does E1 run on a Mac?

Yes. The desktop app runs on Macs with Apple silicon on macOS 12 or newer, signed and notarized by Apple. On an Intel Mac, use E1 in the browser.

Is there a Linux version?

There is no Linux desktop app. E1 runs in the browser on Linux with nothing to install.

Is the browser version limited compared with the desktop app?

No. Both open the same projects on the same server with the same account and tools. The desktop app adds real floating windows that reopen where they were, and its own updates.

Can we use E1 on an iPad or another tablet?

Yes, in the browser. E1 Reviewer for iPad is coming to the App Store; until then, review on a tablet in the browser.

Can the desktop app connect to our own server?

Yes. At the first start, choose Use E1 Cloud or type your organization's server address, for example https://e1.example.com, and click Connect. The app remembers it.

How is the desktop app updated?

It looks for a new version at start and every four hours, downloads it in the background and asks you to restart. Check for Updates… in the app menu looks at once.

Can I be signed in on my laptop and a tablet at once?

Yes. One account may be open in up to three places at once. In a fourth, E1 asks which place to sign out.
