14. Workflow and dashboards

Statuses of objects, sheets, documents and tasks in status sets with your own sub-statuses, the status chip, value statuses (Assumed, Preliminary, Confirmed, Final) and holds; workflows, boards and progress dashboards as they are built.

8 features8 availableGuide updated 2026-11-11

About this guide

Every object, sheet, document and task in E1 has a status. Statuses come in status sets: five fixed categories (New, Active, Resolved, Closed, Removed) with the sub-statuses your company or project adds under them. Values have a status of their own, from Assumed to Final, and anything can be put on hold with a reason.

Typical workflow

  1. Make a status set with your sub-statuses, and choose it for objects and sheets.
  2. Set an object's status on its status chip.
  3. Mark the values you have checked Confirmed; a change makes them Preliminary again.
  4. Put on hold what waits for someone else, and find it in the query view.
  5. Make a workflow from a template, assign it to instruments, and move them on their chip or on the board.

Status sets and the project's statuses

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-statusesRoadmap 33.1Review checks S1

Every object, sheet, document and task has a status from a status set: the five categories New, Active, Resolved, Closed and Removed, with sub-statuses a set adds under them. Configuration → Statuses makes a set and chooses the one each level reads.

Where
Configuration → Workflow → Statuses
Who
Everyone who can view the project sees statuses. Making a project's status set and choosing the sets: Manage project workflows (Project administrator); a company set: Edit the company library. With Editor Basic the section is read only.

Make a status set

#ActionExpected result
1Open Configuration → Statuses and click New status set…. Type its name, for example Deliverable, and tick what it is for: Objects, Sheets.The five categories are listed, each with Add under ….
2Click Add under Active and type Waiting for vendor data.—
3Choose Save to This project (or The company library) and click Save and publish.The set is listed under Status sets with its statuses in order.
4Under The project's status set per level, choose Deliverable for Objects and Sheets.Objects and sheets read it from now on. An item keeps its status; one the set does not have shows its first status, New.
  • The five main statuses cannot be removed or given another category; their names may change. Statuses are kept in category order.
  • Tasks read the task set: Open, In review, Done, Removed. A task is set Open or Done on its row in Tasks.
  • Built-in sets that their topics set are listed too: the revision, markup and construction sets.

The status chip: set an item's status

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-status-chipRoadmap 33.1Review checks S1

Under an object's name the status chip shows its status (its category before it when they differ), HOLD, and No workflow. Click it to set another status of its set.

Where
An object's window, under its name; a sheet's and a document's too
Who
Setting a status: Move items in their workflow (Engineer and above)
#ActionExpected result
1Open a pump's window.Under its name the chip reads New and No workflow.
2Click the chip and choose Waiting for vendor data.The chip reads Active ▸ Waiting for vendor data in the status's colour. The history lists the change.
3Click the chip and choose New.The chip reads New again.
4In the query view add the columns Status and Status category; ask Alex to list the equipment whose status category is Active.The pump is listed with its status.

An item without a status counts as New. Statuses change only through the status chip, the query view and Alex, never as raw edits.

Value statuses: Assumed, Preliminary, Confirmed, Final

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-value-statusRoadmap 33.1Review checks S2

A value carries a status of its own, set from its row's right-click menu and shown by a mark after its name. A Confirmed or Final value that changes becomes Preliminary again. Calculated values have none.

Where
An object's window → Attributes: right-click a value's row
Who
Set value statuses (Engineer and above) and edit attributes
#ActionExpected result
1Right-click the row of a pump's Description and choose Confirmed under Value status.A ✓ after the name; pointing at it reads Value status: Confirmed.
2Type another description.The mark is ◐: the value is Preliminary again.
3Right-click the row and choose Not set.No mark.
  • The marks: ○ Assumed, ◐ Preliminary, ✓ Confirmed, ✔ Final, nothing for Not set.
  • A value calculated by a rule has no status of its own: choosing one is refused.
  • The query view filters by Value status of … through Alex.

Put an item or a value on hold

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-holdRoadmap 33.1Review checks S2

Put on hold… with a reason marks an object, a sheet, a document or one value HOLD, with who and why; Release hold takes it off. A hold never blocks editing; it is a filter and a column in the query view.

Where
The status chip; a value's right-click menu
Who
Put on hold (Lead engineer, Project administrator)
#ActionExpected result
1Click the pump's status chip and choose Put on hold…. Type Waiting for vendor data.HOLD beside the chip; pointing at it says why and who.
2Right-click a value's row and choose Put on hold….HOLD after the value's name. Changing the value keeps the hold.
3In the query view add the column Hold.The held pump shows HOLD: Waiting for vendor data. Ask Alex to list what is on hold: a filter by hold.
4Choose Release hold on the chip and on the value.No HOLD.

Workflows: steps an item moves through

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-workflowsRoadmap 33.2Review checks S3

A workflow is the statuses of a status set in order, its steps, with a credit, a time limit and a lock each may have. Make one from a template (Engineering deliverable, ISO 19650 deliverable, Instrument data, Diagram, Engineering object) in Configuration → Workflows.

Where
Configuration → Workflow → Workflows
Who
Making a project's workflow: Manage project workflows (Project administrator); a company workflow: Edit the company library. With Editor Basic the section is read only.
#ActionExpected result
1Open Configuration → Workflows and choose Instrument data in From template.It is listed: New → In design → Ready for check → Vendor data → Closed (locked), and its status set is under Statuses.
2Ask Alex to change a step, for example to give In design a limit of 10 days and two reasons to move back.Alex saves a new draft; publishing it makes version 2.
  • Each step is a status of the set, once; Removed is never a step, it is always reachable. A step's credit never decreases down the list.
  • A locked step keeps its items' values, name, tag and owner as they are until the item moves back; holds, comments and tasks stay possible. Rules leave a locked item's values as they are.
  • A step may make a task about the item, or set a value, when an item enters it.

Assign workflows and start them

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-assignmentsRoadmap 33.2Review checks S3

Assignments say which workflow the project's items follow: objects by class (then their parent classes, then any class), sheets, documents, tasks. Apply starts the workflow on every item that has not started it, as one change.

Where
Configuration → Workflows → Assignments
Who
Manage project workflows (Project administrator)
#ActionExpected result
1Click Add assignment: Objects, class plant.class.instrument, workflow Instrument data.—
2Click Apply.“Saved; 40 items started their workflow.” Every instrument's chip reads New and Instrument data · 0 days.

Save keeps the assignments without starting anything: an item then shows Start workflow on its chip.

Move an item to another step

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-moveRoadmap 33.2Review checks S3

On the status chip an item with a workflow moves: to the next step, back to an earlier one with a reason, to Removed and back with a reason. A held item does not move forward; in a locked step its values, name and tag wait until it is moved back.

Where
The status chip on an object's window; the board; Alex for many at once
Who
Move items in their workflow (Engineer and above)
#ActionExpected result
1Click an instrument's chip and choose Move to In design.The chip reads Active ▸ In design and Instrument data · 0 days.
2Ask Alex to move the 40 instruments to Ready for check.They move as one change; Alex lists any that did not move and why (on hold, for example).
3On the chip choose Move back to In design… and type a reason.It moves back; the reason is kept and the return is counted.
4Move it on to Closed and type a new description.Refused: it is in a locked step of its workflow. Move it back to change it.
5Choose Remove…, then Restore to …, each with a reason.Removed, then back in the step it left.

The workflow board

AvailableSince 2026-11-11WF-boardRoadmap 33.2Review checks S3

A workflow's items as cards in a column per step and Removed, with their days in the step, returns and HOLD. Drag a card to another column, or use Move to… on it, to move it.

Where
Configuration → Workflows → Open board
Who
Seeing the board: everyone who can view the project; moving cards: Move items in their workflow
#ActionExpected result
1Click Open board on Instrument data.A column per step with its count, and Removed at the right; each card shows its days in the step (red when overdue), how often it came back, and HOLD.
2Drag a card from In design to Ready for check.It moves; the counts change.
3Drag it back, or choose In design in its Move to….E1 asks why, then moves it back.

A column shows at most 200 cards, then how many more there are.
