Privacy policy.

What personal data we process, why, and your rights. This website uses no tracking and no advertising cookies.

1. Who is responsible

The controller under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is Lanxtor Inc., 7545 Irvine Center Drive, Suite 200, Irvine, California 92618, USA, email info@lanxtor.com. See the legal notice for all company details.

2. Visiting this website

When you open a page, the hosting service receives the technical data your browser sends: IP address, date and time, the page requested, the referring page, and browser and operating system. It is used only to deliver the website and keep it secure, and kept in server logs only as long as needed for that, then deleted.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. Cookies and browser storage

This website only stores what it needs to work. It sets no tracking, analytics or advertising cookies, and does not load fonts or scripts from other companies.

  • Language and currency: the language and currency you choose, so the next page shows the same (browser storage, until you clear it).
  • Signed-in hint: whether to show "Account" or "Log in" in the menu (browser storage).
  • Sign-in session: a secure cookie from app.lanxtor.com when you sign in to E1, to keep you signed in (until you sign out or it expires).
  • Previews and votes: your progress in the Academy preview and your votes on the feature board, kept in your browser only.
  • Cookie notice: that you have seen the cookie notice (browser storage).

These are strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so no consent is needed (Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive, § 25(2) TDDDG in Germany). You can delete them at any time in your browser settings. If we ever add optional cookies, we will ask for your consent first.

4. Your E1 account

When you create an account and use E1, we process your name, email address, organization, licenses, usage, Academy progress and certificates, and the content you create. Sign-in is provided by Microsoft Entra External ID. We use this data to provide E1, bill your organization and support you.

Legal basis: the contract with you or your organization (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Data is kept while the account exists and then as long as tax and commercial law requires.

Usage statistics, only if you agree. E1 asks each person once whether it may share usage statistics with Lanxtor Inc., and you can change your answer at any time in E1 under Settings → Usage statistics. Only if you agree, E1 counts per day how long you actively work in E1 (only while you click or type), how many clicks and key presses, which functions you use and which places you open. It never records what you draw, type or name. We use these counts to see which parts of E1 help and which need work, and to support your organization. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Stopping deletes what was shared.

5. Contacting us

When you email or call us, we use your details to answer you and, for sales enquiries, to prepare an offer. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR. We delete the messages when they are no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep them.

6. The Alex assistant

When you ask Alex, the assistant on this website, your questions and the conversation so far are sent to the Azure OpenAI Service in our Microsoft Azure account, which writes the answer. Do not enter personal or confidential information. The conversation is kept only in this browser tab. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering questions about our products (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Microsoft processes the data for us as a processor under our data processing agreement and does not use it to train AI models.

7. Who receives data

Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited hosts the website and the E1 Cloud for us as a processor under a data processing agreement, in Microsoft Azure data centres in the EU. Where data may reach Microsoft outside the EU, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the EU standard contractual clauses. We do not sell personal data.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, to data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to info@lanxtor.com.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example where you live: in Germany the authority of your federal state, in France the CNIL, in Spain the AEPD, in Norway Datatilsynet, and in the United Kingdom the ICO.

9. Changes

We update this policy when our website or services change.
