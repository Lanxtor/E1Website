E1 user guide

How to use E1, feature by feature: where each feature is, what to do step by step, what you should see, and whether it is available today or still being built.

379 features333 available17 under development11 planned3 placeholder11 preview data only4 parkedGuide updated 2026-11-11

Every guide describes one feature the way you meet it on screen: where to find it, who may use it, what to do step by step, and what you should see after each step. The labels in bold are the exact words on the screen. Each feature carries its status and the date it was delivered or last changed, so you always know whether you are reading about something you can use today.

The screenshots were taken from E1 on 2026-11-07, in the default dark appearance, with a small demo project (a pump train on one sheet).

1. Getting started

The E1 window: menus, the left rail, the explorer, tabs, the side panel, the status bar, keyboard shortcuts, your settings and Help.

2. Installation and sign-in

Open E1 in the browser or the desktop app, sign in with your company account or Microsoft, passkeys, sessions and the store E1 runs on.

3. Projects and the explorer

Create, rename, arrange and delete projects and folders, the sections of a project, the Bin, searching and dragging in the explorer.

4. Objects, attributes and tags

Make equipment and other objects from library classes, fill in attributes with units, relate objects, and tag them with your project's rules.

21 features · 21 available

5. Drawing P&IDs

The sheet view: place symbols, draw pipes, signals and cables, select, move, format, layers, flow, off-page connectors and loop diagrams.

47 features · 45 available

6. Lists and queries

Any list is a live query of the model: columns, filters, grouping, editing like Excel, templates, and Excel export and import.

25 features · 25 available

7. Documents, revisions and datasheets

Documents and their revisions, issuing, changed since, PDF and Excel output, sheet PDF and printing, datasheets and the datasheet designer.

36 features · 35 available

8. Working together

History and undo, comments and mentions, tasks and the Inbox, comments pinned on drawings, attachments and live updates between people.

16 features · 12 available

9. Organization, members and access

Your organization, invitations, licenses and seats, project members and roles, exporting the organization, and the access control being built.

26 features · 25 available

10. Configuration, library and catalogs

The Configuration place: classes, attributes, shapes, the company library with drafts, publishing and upgrades, catalogs, content packages and project settings.

32 features · 30 available

11. AI: Alex

Alex, the built-in engineering co-worker that reviews your drawing and, with help from a language model when you tick it, answers in your own words and changes the project with review and undo.

36 features · 35 available

12. Your account, Academy and website

The account page on lanxtor.com: licenses, usage, organizations, projects, billing, downloads, the Academy with courses and certificates, Shape E1 and Alex on the website.

22 features · 7 available

13. Wiring (EI&C)

Cabinets and junction boxes in their locations, rails, terminal strips and terminals, I/O cards and channels, cables and their cores landed on terminals, tracing a circuit from the field to the I/O channel, construction status, and cable lengths.

17 features · 17 available

14. Workflow and dashboards

Statuses of objects, sheets, documents and tasks in status sets with your own sub-statuses, the status chip, value statuses (Assumed, Preliminary, Confirmed, Final) and holds; workflows, boards and progress dashboards as they are built.

22 features · 22 available

15. Cause and effect, safety functions, alarms

Interlocks with their causes and effects as the cause and effect matrix and its narratives, safety instrumented functions with their required SIL and PFD, alarms rationalized and suppressed by plant state, and the matrix and alarm list as documents.

5 features · 5 available

16. E1 Reviewer for iPad

Review E1 projects on the iPad: dashboards, projects, sheets, objects, photos and voice notes, redlining, your tasks and checklists, search and Alex.

What the statuses mean

Available
In E1 today. You can follow the steps and should see what is described.
Under development
Being built now. The guide describes the planned behavior; labels may still change.
Planned
Decided and scheduled on the roadmap, not started.
Placeholder
The control is on screen but opens Under construction instead of doing something.
Preview data only
On the website, shown with preview data until the E1 cloud answers it.
Parked
Stopped by decision; not offered and may never be built.
